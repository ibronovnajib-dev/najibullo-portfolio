CASE STUDY

Product case study

01 / USTOHONA.TJ

From finding a master to a complete service marketplace.

Ustohona.tj brings customers and masters into one product flow: category discovery, free job posting, offers, profiles, direct contact and post-job ratings.

100% freeCore platform access
Jobs + offersTwo-sided marketplace flow
1–5 starsReview and rating system
Direct contactCustomer ↔ master connection
01CHALLENGE

Trust and discovery are fragmented.

Customers need a clear path from a household problem to a credible local specialist.

02ARCHITECTURE

Two-sided marketplace flow.

Categories → request → offers → profile → chat/call → completion → review, with separate customer and master journeys.

03DECISIONS

Trust and direct action first.

Core access stays free, communication stays direct, reputation uses 1–5★ reviews, and the interface is designed mobile-first.

04DIFFICULTIES

Two different users, one coherent product.

Customer and master journeys need different actions while sharing the same trust and communication layer.

05SOLUTION

A focused end-to-end mobile flow.

Discovery, requests, offers, profiles, messaging and calls are connected as one understandable service journey.

06OUTCOME

A live service marketplace.

A working customer ↔ master product with documented real UI and verified product facts.

PRODUCT

Marketplace logic

Search by profession and area, publish a request, compare offers and move directly into contact.

TRUST

Profiles & reputation

Ratings, comments and work evidence create a clearer trust layer between customers and masters.

FOUNDER BUILD

End-to-end ownership

Product structure, responsive interface, feature logic and launch preparation are presented as one system.

01PROBLEM

Useful digital services are scattered.

People need a clearer way to discover social, work, commerce, travel and utility services without jumping across unrelated products.

02ROLE

Founder + product architecture.

I define the ecosystem structure, information architecture, localization model, public product layer and technical presentation.

03ARCHITECTURE

34 service areas under one system.

The product groups many journeys into a shared taxonomy with modular discovery and a common multilingual information layer.

04DECISIONS

One taxonomy, three languages.

Tajik, Russian and English content, structured public pages and search-friendly documentation keep the ecosystem understandable at scale.

05BUILD

Discovery, content and platform readiness.

The current package includes the service catalog, localized content, responsive behavior, accessibility constraints and SEO/AI-facing documentation.

06RESULT

A structured multi-service product.

The documented result is 34 service areas, three languages and a coherent discovery system. Usage metrics remain unpublished until verified.

ARCHITECTURE

One system, many journeys

Social content, marketplace, work, mobility, property, auto, tourism and utility services are documented under one structure.

DISCOVERY

Structured for people and agents

Public information pages, sitemap/robots/LLM files and localized content make the ecosystem easier to understand and index.

RESPONSIVE

Forum-ready presentation

The product package includes mobile, accessibility and reduced-motion constraints for reliable public demonstration.
